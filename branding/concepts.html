Habit Assistant — logo concepts

A. Check bubble (chosen: logo.svg)

A chat bubble with a check in it: say it, and it's done. The two ideas the app is built on, in two bold shapes.

B. Streak dots

A "typing…" bubble whose dots grow left to right, like a streak building up. Friendly, but reads as a generic chat app, and the dots blur together below 32px.

C. Ring check

A round speech bubble drawn as a completed progress ring, with a check inside. Lighter and more "tracker"-like, but the outline thins out at favicon size.
